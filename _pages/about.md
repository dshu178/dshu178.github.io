With 20+ years of experience in the silicon and communications industry, I have built both capability and capacity with a strong focus on delivering results. I am a true innovator with deep industry connections and a passionate linchpin who quickly connects the dots to identify new opportunities.

I excel at taking on brand-new challenges with a fresh perspective. Throughout my career, I have worked at a wide range of leading companies, including [Marvell](https://www.marvell.com/), [Qualcomm](https://www.qualcomm.com/home), [Broadcom](https://www.broadcom.com/), [AMD](https://www.amd.com/en.html), [Semtech/Gennum](https://www.semtech.com/products/signal-integrity), [HP/Agilent/Keysight](https://www.keysight.com/us/en/home.html). My work has spanned diverse fields such as Wi-Fi, wireless communications, RF front-end, optical networking, high-speed SerDes, multicore CPU/GPU architectures, low-power design, and mixed-signal systems.


This is not one year of experience repeated 20+ times. My diverse experience in architect roles has given me valuable insights and the ability to connect seemingly unrelated ideas. Below is a condensed list of the value I bring to the industry:

- My [M-shaped expertise](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/which-letter-shaped-employee-you-gaurav-sharma/), combined with a continuous learning and growth mindset, makes me highly effective in architect roles.
- My analog, mixed-signal, RF, and mmWave experience covers frequencies ranging from DC to 120 GHz.
- My architect-role experience spans from silicon backend operations all the way to system-level - frontend concept design.
- My data analysis skills and experience drive data-informed decision-making.
- I think outside the box and challenge conventional assumptions.

I graduated from [Xi'an Jiaotong University](http://en.xjtu.edu.cn/) and pursued postgraduate studies at [University of Electronic Science and Technology of China](https://en.uestc.edu.cn/)
